Privacy Policy

DigitalZikr: Tasbeeh Counter · Last updated: October 7, 2026

In short: we don't collect, store or share any of your personal data. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

Data we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We have no servers that receive your information.

Data stored on your device

To work, the app saves the following only on your phone:

  • Your counts, targets, daily goal, statistics and streaks
  • Your custom dhikr, favourites and sequences
  • Your settings (language, theme, tasbeeh device, sounds, reminders)

This data never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Location

If you turn on prayer-time reminders or Ramadan iftar times, the app asks for your approximate location once to calculate prayer times. The location is stored only on your device and is never sent to us or anyone else. You can refuse or revoke this permission at any time in your phone's Settings; the counter keeps working without it.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No notification data is sent to any server.

Purchases

The optional one-time "DigitalZikr Pro" purchase is processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment details. We only learn from the store whether Pro is unlocked on your device.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not collect any information from children or adults.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
